The app

Bibleca has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network connections. We receive no data from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

The chapters you have marked as read, your marks and notes, your settings, and a photo you choose for the verse widget are stored only on your iPhone, for the app and its widgets. They are part of your iPhone’s backup. We cannot see them.

Photos

When you choose a photo for the widget, iOS gives the app only that photo. A copy is kept on your iPhone so the widget can show it.

The App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates and may collect data under its own privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted, and you may complain to a supervisory authority. Ours is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

Die Kapitel, die du als gelesen markiert hast, deine Markierungen und Notizen, deine Einstellungen und ein Foto, das du für das Vers-Widget wählst, werden nur auf deinem iPhone gespeichert, für die App und ihre Widgets. Sie sind Teil des Backups deines iPhones. Wir können sie nicht sehen.

Fotos
